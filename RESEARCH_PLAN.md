# Suggested Research Paper

**Title:** Wall-Scraper Rate: A Park-Transportability Measure of Major League Home Run Production

1. Abstract: define the problem, method, coverage, and primary findings.
2. Introduction: why raw HR totals do not fully represent portable power.
3. Related work: Statcast expected HR, park factors, and batted-ball flight modeling.
4. Data: Statcast HRs 2015–2026, inclusion rules, missingness, park seasons.
5. Methods: trajectory model, park geometry, neutralization, 25-stadium threshold.
6. Validation: benchmark against observed outcomes and expected-HR resources.
7. Results: WSR leaders, team/park effects, year trends, hitter archetypes.
8. Case studies: short-porch beneficiaries, road/home splits, traded hitters.
9. Sensitivity: alternate thresholds (e.g. 3/5/7 parks), weather policy, uncertainty.
10. Limitations: tracking gaps, geometry changes, model assumptions.
11. Conclusion: interpretability and front-office/broadcast use.

Recommended figures:
- WSR distribution by season
- Wall-Scraper vs No-Doubter share by hitter
- Park-by-park counterfactual HR matrix
- Spray-angle vs wall-distance heatmap
- Player's actual HR vs standardized 30-park HR
- Classification uncertainty / model coverage plot
